Method: how the engine works

One deterministic engine. 2,160 hourly ticks (90 days). Tickets arrive by region and hour, split into topics × country tiers × two personas (enterprise HR/Finance admins and employees). Seeded random numbers; every model sees identical demand. Results are averaged over several seeds with P10–P90 ranges.

Four coupled feedback loops.

  • Deflection ↔ liability. Lower AI confidence gate = more deflection but more wrong answers on regulated topics, which cost reopens, exposure and account health.
  • Middleman bottleneck. Payroll/Legal/Compliance are finite, daytime-only pools. Without structured intake, agents burn minutes pinging them and tickets sit in queue.
  • Burnout & coaching collapse. Sustained overload raises burnout and attrition, pulls managers from coaching to firefighting, and quality drops weeks later.
  • Silent employee churn. Slow or wrong answers to employees depress account health even when the enterprise admin’s tickets meet SLA.

Governance in the loop. The playbook’s kill criterion watches rolling audited error and automatically halves AI scope for a week when it trips.

Limits. This is an illustrative model, not Multiplier data. Use it to compare strategies and see sensitivities, not to forecast exact values.
